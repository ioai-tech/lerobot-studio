Segments gain `excluded`, a host fact for a rejected range. The viewer draws an excluded segment greyed and hatched with the Excluded text on the track, in the list and in the inspector, never as confirmed or to review whatever its review state, and leaves it out of `N / M confirmed` (shown as its own count), the To review filter and `Shift+A`. The segment list offers the Issues filter only when it narrows the list (some segments have an issue, some do not); a hidden filter shows All.
